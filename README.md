## 👋 Who we are

**milzer GmbH** is a German traveltech company that has been building software for the tourism industry since **2011**. We work with specialist tour operators, OTAs and DMCs — companies whose products are too individual for off-the-shelf tools and whose processes deserve better than spreadsheets and manual data entry.

What we do comes down to three things:

| | |
|---|---|
| 🧭 **Consulting** | We analyse existing system landscapes, check them against business goals and help choose the right path forward. |
| 🛠️ **Development** | We design and build tailor-made software for tour operators — and our own **Flow** product family. |
| 🔌 **System integration** | We connect travel systems via APIs so data flows automatically, completely and reliably. |

## ✨ The Flow platform

Our products share one idea: **travel data should flow** — from supplier to operator to travel agency, without copy‑paste in between.

### FlowCore — the operating system for modern tour operators

FlowCore is our cloud travel ERP. It brings product management, bookings, finance and AI agents together on a single platform.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

- 🗺️ **Trips & departures** — itineraries, dates, price matrices and seasons, multilingual
- 📋 **Bookings & operations** — bookings, flights and follow-ups in one process, with full history
- 💶 **Finance & commissions** — invoices, payments, bank import (MT940), commission accounts
- 🏨 **Inventory** — accommodation & suppliers, allotments, Giata hotel data
- 👥 **CRM** — contacts, agencies, tags, custom fields and commission rules

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

- 🖼️ **Content & PIM** — product content and images for website, catalogue and sales
- 📊 **Dashboards** — sales and occupancy KPIs, shareable across teams
- 🤖 **AI agents** — search and check real data, prepare changes, execute only after human approval
- 🔐 **Enterprise-ready** — fine-grained roles, multi-tenancy, session management, DE / EN / ES
- 🧩 **API-first** — REST API, MCP server, API keys and webhooks

</td>
</tr>
</table>

### The rest of the family

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **FlowConnect** | Integration hub linking tour operators, travel agencies and service providers — including automated mid-office data transfer to systems such as Midoco, myJACK, Argus and Bosys. |
| **FlowDiscover** | Makes round trips easy to find and book for travel agencies — right inside their mid-office, showing only products that are actually bookable. |

## 📦 Open source

|---|---|
